# Veloura — Premium Fashion Storefront

A polished, responsive storefront demo for a modern clothing brand. Veloura pairs an editorial fashion aesthetic with a complete front-end shopping journey, from browsing a seasonal collection to viewing an order confirmation.

> **Demo notice:** This is a front-end experience only. Orders and cash-on-delivery checkout are simulated; no order is sent to a server and no real payment is collected.

## Highlights

- **Editorial storefront design** — Refined typography, warm neutral colors, fashion photography, and a seasonal collection hero.
- **Responsive shopping experience** — Layouts adapt across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
- **Three-piece fashion collection** — Browse product imagery, descriptions, and prices, then select a size (S, M, or L).
- **Interactive shopping bag** — Add items by size, see the live item count and subtotal, remove items, and review your bag in an animated side panel.
- **Guided checkout flow** — Enter delivery details, review the order summary, and continue to a dedicated cash-on-delivery demo page.
- **Order confirmation screen** — See a demo order reference, purchased items, delivery details, and order total after submitting checkout.
- **About and contact sections** — Learn the brand story and find the displayed studio and contact information.
- **Motion and feedback** — Smooth section transitions, animated product cards and cart drawer, plus toast messages for shopping actions.
- **Newsletter sign-up UI** — Try the footer subscription form and see its demo confirmation feedback.

## Shopping Journey

1. Explore the collection from the homepage.
2. Choose a size for a product and add it to your shopping bag.
3. Review items, quantities, and subtotal in the bag.
4. Enter delivery details and continue to checkout.
5. Submit the cash-on-delivery demo form to view the order confirmation.
